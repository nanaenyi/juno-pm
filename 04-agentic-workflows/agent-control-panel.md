## What Juno Is Doing

In the Prioritizing Risks pillar, Juno scans each week’s Slack, Jira and Notion signals and drafts the Monday Risk Radar: the top 3 cited risks, ranked by RocketShip’s P0–P3 framework, for the PM to review before standup.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

max_steps: 50 tool calls per run.
Abort early if Slack and Jira are both unreachable after 2 retries each, because there’s no basis for a ranking. If one source fails 3 times in a row (the first try plus 2 retries), Juno skips that source, adds a “source unavailable” banner and continues, rather than aborting.
Hard timeout: 5 minutes wall clock for the scheduled Monday run, and 60 seconds for a mid-sprint rerun. When a limit is hit, Juno ships what has passed verification, with a “partial scan” banner.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Slack search: on success, returns {status: ok, threads: [ids], count: N, window: 7d}. On failure, returns {status: error, code}. A count of 0 on a 7-day window counts as “failed,” because RocketShip’s channels are never silent for a week.
Jira query: on success, returns {status: ok, tickets: [keys], count: N, filter: "P0/P1 or changed this week"}. On failure, returns {status: error, code}. A count of 0 is “suspect,” not “no risks”: Juno retries once, then flags it.
Notion page read: on success, returns {status: ok, page_id, last_edited: date}. On failure, returns {status: error}. A page with last_edited older than 30 days comes back as {status: stale}.
CRM snapshot read: on success, returns {status: ok, accounts_flagged: N, as_of: date}. A snapshot older than 7 days comes back as {status: stale}.
Citation check (internal): returns {claim_id, source_id, match: true/false}. A false match is a failure, never “close enough.”

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

Juno never posts or sends at any confidence level. Confidence only decides where a risk appears on the PM’s screen.

High: 2 or more independent sources (e.g. a Slack thread plus a Jira ticket) agree, every number appears verbatim in a source, and the P0–P3 rank fits the One-Pager definitions. The risk appears as a ranked row in the top 3.
Medium: only 1 source, or sources partly conflict on a date or number. The risk appears, but tagged NEEDS CLARIFICATION, and its suggested next step is “ask the owner.”
Low: no source contains the claim, or a check failed. The risk is moved to “Held back: unverified” with the reason shown. It’s never ranked and never dropped silently.

**North Star** (Context):

Always surface the 3 risks that most deserve the PM’s attention this week, each backed by a Slack, Jira or Notion citation. Never invent a customer name, ARR figure or contract term, and never send, post or assign anything.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno can decide alone to read the approved sources, rank risks, tag items NEEDS CLARIFICATION, move items to “Held back” or “Routed to you,” and draft the standup note. It can only propose, for PM approval, adding a new source, changing the ranking rules, or saving a PM-edited row to memory. It can never post to Slack, send email or Intercom messages, assign or edit Jira tickets, or draft customer, press or contract replies. Those are handed to the PM.

**Access Control:**

Juno reads with the PM’s own permissions, so it sees only the Slack channels, Jira projects and Notion pages the PM can see. That means no private DMs, HR or billing data, or customer contact details. It’s read-only everywhere except its own Radar screen. Its access to the CRM covers only the Sales Pipeline Snapshot’s blocker and risk flags, not contact records. It gets no access at all to the reporting database, per the Engineering Capacity Note’s ban on new load until sharding ships.

**Fallback Protocols:**

One source fails 3 times: Juno skips it, shows “[Source] unavailable, risks may be missing,” and tags any row depending on it NEEDS CLARIFICATION.
Slack and Jira both down: no ranking. Juno shows the outage notice plus last week’s open items, labeled as last week’s.
50-call or timeout limit hit: Juno stops and ships the rows that passed verification, with a “partial scan” banner. It never makes a 51st call or quietly extends the timeout.
Fewer than 3 rows pass verification: Juno shows only those, with “Only N risks met the evidence bar this week,” and never pads the list.
Every fallback is logged in the run summary at the bottom of the screen.

**Checkpoints:**

Before standup: the PM reviews the whole Radar before anything from it is used. Nothing is shared until the PM copies it.
Any “Routed to you” item: contract, legal, regulator, press or customer-comms items stop at the PM, and Juno takes no further action on them.
Before memory saves a PM correction: the PM confirms the edit, so a mis-click doesn’t become next week’s truth.
Before any new source or ranking-rule change: PM approval is required.
