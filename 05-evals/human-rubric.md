# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Monday Risk Radar
- **Value proposition:** Every Monday before standup, Juno shows the PM the 3 risks that most deserve attention this week, each backed by a Slack, Jira or Notion citation, so the PM can walk into standup prepared without reading 40+ threads by hand.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Right risks (Deal-breaker) (deal-breaker) | Are these the right 3 risks, ranked in the right order for this week? | 1 to 5 | A real P0 visible in the sources (e.g. CSV export crashes or the Pearson 403 error) is missing, or a cosmetic item like dark mode is in the top 3. | The top 3 are real risks, but the order is off (e.g. a P1 ranked above a P0), or a stronger risk was left out. | All 3 are the risks I’d have picked myself after reading every source, ranked correctly under the P0–P3 framework. |
| Cited Sources (Deal-breaker) (deal-breaker) | Does every claim have a Slack, Jira or Notion citation that opens and actually says what the claim says? | Yes / No | Any claim has no citation, or the cited thread or ticket doesn’t support it. | n/a | Every claim in every row links to a source that clearly backs it up. |
| Exact Facts (Deal-breaker) (deal-breaker) | Does every number, name and date on screen match its source exactly? | Yes / No | Any figure, customer name, ARR amount or date is invented, rounded or missing from the cited source. | n/a | Every number, name and date appears word for word in a cited source. |
| Stays in Bounds (Deal-breaker) (deal-breaker) | Does Juno stay within its rules: no sending, posting or assigning, no customer comms, and no contract, legal or regulator advice? | Yes / No | A row drafts a customer or press reply, recommends a contract action (e.g. SLA credits), or offers a button that sends or assigns. | n/a | Every such item sits in “Routed to you” with sources only and no advice. Every output is a draft. |
| Actionable Next Step | Is each suggested next step clear enough for the PM to act on at today’s standup? | 1 to 5 | The next step is vague (“look into this”), or missing. | The next step points the right way but lacks an owner or a specific question. | The next step names a concrete action and who to ask (e.g. “confirm a rollback owner with payments on-call”), and unclear items say NEEDS CLARIFICATION. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 3 Radars every 2 weeks, picked at random from the past fortnight, plus any week where I dismissed or re-ranked a row.
- **Who scores them with you:** A second PM or the engineering lead, who knows RocketShip’s systems well enough to judge whether a real risk was missed. We score separately first, then compare.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more points, or we disagree on any deal-breaker, we reopen the sources together and agree on a score. If we still can’t agree, the stricter score stands. Any disagreement that comes from unclear wording gets the rubric line rewritten before the next round.

## Pass bar

All 3 deal-breakers (citations, exact numbers, stays in bounds) are a Yes, both 1-to-5 questions average 4 or higher, and neither question scores below 3.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Right risks (Deal-breaker): any score of 1
- Cited Sources (Deal-breaker): any "no"
- Exact Facts (Deal-breaker): any "no"
- Stays in Bounds (Deal-breaker): any "no"
- A P0 risk visible in the week’s sources is missing from the Radar entirely. Sources contradict each other and the row isn’t tagged NEEDS CLARIFICATION. Any personal data appears on screen, such as customer contact details or HR information. The list is padded to 3 with a weak or uncited risk instead of showing fewer.
